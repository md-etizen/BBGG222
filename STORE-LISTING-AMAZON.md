## Product description
Keep every monthly bill in one cheerful place. BillBubbles lets you log rent, electricity, gas, utilities, insurance and anything else, month by month, then shows you where your money goes. A built-in Tips tab does the same for your work tips.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Add a bill for any month with a couple of taps, or add many months at once
• Set due dates and repeats (weekly, every two weeks, monthly or yearly) and see them on a calendar
• Name any bill, and pick its icon and color from a big icon collection
• See a running total for each month and how it compares with last month
• Find your most expensive and cheapest months, your average, and which category costs the most
• See a reminder at the top when a bill is due soon
• Get heads-up alerts when a bill jumps above your usual, plus simple ways to save
• Explore colorful charts: trends, category breakdowns, what changed and when bills are due

TIPS TAB
• Enter the tips you made and the hours you worked for any day
• Your month grid shows each day's total and the hourly rate it works out to
• See tips this month, hours worked, your average hourly rate and your best day

MAKE IT YOURS
• Five color themes plus light, dark or automatic mode

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything stays on your device. No account, no ads and no tracking.
