BillBubbles Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

In short: BillBubbles does not collect, send or share any of your information.

What you enter

Everything you enter is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage: your bills (categories, custom names, icons, colors, amounts, notes, months, due dates and repeat settings), the tips and hours you log in the Tips tab, and your settings (theme, dark mode and reminders). We never receive any of it and cannot see it.

What we do not do

BillBubbles has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking tools. The app does not use the internet to send your data anywhere. It does not ask for access to your contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone. The only permission it can ask for is to show notifications, and only if you switch on bill reminders. Those reminders are created and shown on your device; nothing about them is sent to us or anyone else.

Backups

If you back up your device (for example to iCloud or to a computer), the app's data may be included in that backup. That is controlled by your device settings and the backup provider's own policies, not by us.

Deleting your data

You can delete any bill or tip entry inside the app. Deleting the app from your device removes all of its data.

Children

BillBubbles is not directed at children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, no information is collected from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
